# Video Game Sales Analysis (1980-2020)

## Project Overview
This data analytics project analyzes 40+ years of video game sales data to answer the question: **"What are the key factors that drive success in the global video game market?"** The analysis explores market leadership, regional preferences, and industry trends to provide actionable insights for game developers and publishers.

## Key Research Questions
1. **Market Leadership:** Which publishers dominate and what strategies drive their success?
2. **Regional Dynamics:** How are sales distributed across North America, Europe, Japan, and other regions?
3. **Industry Trends:** When were the most successful periods and what drove these peaks?

## Key Findings

### Market Concentration
- **Nintendo leads** with $1.78B in total sales across 696 games ($2.56M average per game)
- Top 5 publishers (Nintendo, EA, Activision, Sony, Ubisoft) dominate the market
- **First-party exclusives and iconic franchises** drive long-term success

### Regional Preferences
- **North America:** 49.1% of global sales – the primary market
- **Europe:** 27.3% – strong second market
- **Japan:** 14.6% – unique preference for RPGs
- **Insight:** Successful publishers tailor games to regional preferences

### Industry Cycles
- **Peak Year:** 2008 (Wii/PS3/Xbox 360 era)
- Sales closely tied to **console generation launches**
- Predictable market patterns around hardware cycles

### Genre Performance
- **Action games** dominate globally ($1.72B)
- Japan shows distinct preference for **Role-Playing Games**

## 🛠️ Technologies Used
